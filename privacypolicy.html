Privacy Policy – pypyradio

Last updated: March 2026

pypyradio is a free and open‑source application. Your privacy is respected and protected.

1. Data Collection

pypyradio does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal or sensitive user data.

  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or tracking is used
  • No device identifiers are accessed
  • No location data is accessed
  • No data is shared with third parties

2. Permissions

pypyradio does not request any special or dangerous Android permissions. Only basic system access required for core functionality is used.

3. Data Storage

pypyradio does not store any user data on external servers or cloud services. Any temporary data, if used, remains entirely on the user’s device.

4. Third‑Party Services

pypyradio does not use any third‑party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking services.

If the app is downloaded from Google Play, Google may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising

pypyradio does not display advertisements and does not use ad services.

6. Children’s Privacy

pypyradio is safe for all age groups and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Open Source

pypyradio is an open‑source project. The source code is publicly available and can be reviewed to verify the privacy claims made in this policy.
